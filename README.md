# The Long Roast: Lawmakers and the File They Cannot Convert

> "They can draft a 400-page bill to regulate artificial intelligence, but ask them to turn a Word document into a PDF and suddenly the future of technology needs a staffer, a tutorial, and a prayer."

One satirical sentence roasting lawmakers who would regulate AI, crypto or the internet but cannot convert a file to PDF. Entered in the make.fun bounty "The Long Roast: Lawmakers and the File They Cannot Convert".
